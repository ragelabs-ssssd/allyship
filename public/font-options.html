Institutional Philosophy & Mandate

The ALLYSHIP++ Thesis

Where Relationships Precede Recruitment — Bridging Three Decades of Academic Inquiry with the Leaders Shaping India’s Future.

A national initiative by Siksha ‘O’ Anusandhan that brings corporate leaders, academic thinkers, and national icons into a shared conversation—building enduring relationships, mentoring emerging talent, and shaping the future of work.

The Origin & Mandate

Bridging Two Worlds: The Genesis of ALLYSHIP

For too long, the relationship between universities and industry has been limited to annual visits and short interviews. ALLYSHIP was founded on a simple realization: preparing future generations requires continuous conversation, not an annual transaction.

Instead of waiting behind campus gates, Siksha ‘O’ Anusandhan stepped directly into the boardrooms of Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai, and Chennai to listen first. We asked a fundamental question: How do we create workplaces where emerging talent doesn’t just adapt, but genuinely thrives?

The Living Standard · Practicing Allyship

Culture is What Happens on a Tuesday

By bringing Olympic coaches and sports icons alongside corporate leaders, we ground mentorship in the timeless truth of sport: potential only turns into excellence when someone believes in you enough to guide you through the setbacks.

“A diversity policy says what an organization intends on paper. Allyship is what happens on an ordinary Tuesday—who is heard in a meeting, who is trusted with a challenging project, and who speaks up when an early-career colleague is overlooked.”
